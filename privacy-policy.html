Shrinkwrap — Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026 · Applies to the Shrinkwrap Android app

The short version. Shrinkwrap compresses and converts video on your phone. Your videos never leave your device — the app has no servers, no account, and no upload. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and you control that through the consent screen.

Who we are

Shrinkwrap is published by Somethin' Brooklyn ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: privacy@somethinbrooklyn.com.

What the app does with your videos and audio

  • All processing — reading, cropping, trimming, mixing, encoding — happens on your device using Android's own media libraries.
  • Files you choose are accessed only while you are working with them, through Android's photo picker or document picker. The app does not scan or index your library.
  • Finished files are saved to the Movies/Shrinkwrap or Music/Shrinkwrap folders on your device. They are yours; the app keeps no copy anywhere else.
  • A list of your recent conversions (file name, size, date) is stored locally on your device so the Home screen can show it. You can clear it at any time from the Home screen. It is never transmitted.
  • Nothing you create, select, or edit is sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising

Shrinkwrap is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (which you can reset or disable in Android settings under Google → Ads);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

This data is collected and used by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive personal data from Google; we see only aggregated ad performance figures.

Ads are shown only on the app's home screen and, at most once every few minutes, after a conversion completes. Ads never interrupt a conversion in progress.

Your consent choices

Where required by law (including the EU/EEA, the UK, and certain US states), the app shows a consent screen before any ad is requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can choose whether to allow personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time using the Privacy options link on the app's home screen.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet — used solely for advertising. Your videos are never uploaded.
  • Notifications — to show progress for a conversion running in the background. Optional; the app works without it.
  • Foreground service — so a long conversion keeps running if you switch apps.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or your full photo library.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling of personal data.
  • No processing of your media on any server.

Children

Shrinkwrap is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Data retention

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Your conversion history lives only on your device and is removed when you clear it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google's advertising SDK is retained by Google according to its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal data about you. Because we do not hold any, these rights are exercised through your device settings (advertising ID) and through Google for advertising data. If you believe we hold data about you, contact us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example if an analytics tool is added — this page will be updated and the effective date changed. The current version is always at this address.
